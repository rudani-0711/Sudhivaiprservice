GENERAL FAQS

Common questions, answered plainly.

Does Suvidha guarantee my patent will be granted?+
No. Grant or registration is decided solely by the relevant government office. Suvidha assists with search, drafting and filing, not the outcome.
Are government fees included in Suvidha's charges?+
No. Statutory fees paid to the Patent or Copyright Office are separate from Suvidha's professional service charges, and both are itemised before you confirm a filing.
How long does patent protection last?+
Generally 20 years from the filing date, provided renewal fees are paid on schedule.
Do I need a prior-art search before drafting?+
Yes. It sets realistic expectations on novelty before time is spent on a full specification.
Is my uploaded information kept confidential?+
Enquiry and case information is treated as confidential and is only used to progress your filing. This demo prototype does not implement production-grade security — see the note on the Client Login page.
